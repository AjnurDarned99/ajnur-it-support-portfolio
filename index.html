TROUBLESHOOTING MINDSET

Methodical, safe, and user-focused.

The goal is not to guess. It's to understand the impact, perform safe checks, record evidence and escalate with useful context when needed.

01

Clarify the issue

Ask what changed, when it started, who is affected and what the user expected to happen.

02

Check the basics

Confirm scope, connection, error text, recent changes and whether the problem can be reproduced.

03

Test one change

Use low-risk checks first. Change one thing at a time and verify whether the symptom changes.

04

Record & escalate

Document evidence and results. Escalate when access, risk, permissions or expertise require it.

!
Security-first support

Never ask users to share passwords, one-time codes, full payment-card details or private keys. Follow the organisation's approved verification and escalation procedures.
